# Figure captions (v2, checked against the plots)

**Fig. 1.** Fourier amplitude spectra of the X and Y components of the eleven ground-motion pairs after scaling to 35 cm/s²; the green band is the tunable range of the damper (0.3204–0.3542 Hz) and the dotted line marks the second mode of the building (1.011 Hz).

**Fig. 2.** El Centro 1940, nominal building: (a) roof displacement in X without control, with the original RS-TLCD and with the constant pre-set RS-TLCD; (b) Fourier amplitude of the roof displacement (response spectrum), the green band is the tunable range; (c) reduction of the six indices for the two RS-TLCD variants.

**Fig. 3.** El Centro 1940, nominal building: time history of the three actuators (damper frequency, orifice opening κ, rotation angle) for the original RS-TLCD and for the constant pre-set RS-TLCD (0.3486 Hz, 45°: the cell with the highest mean label score of the training data without El Centro 1940, Section 5.4).

**Fig. 4.** Mean reduction of the six indices for each of the eleven records (nominal building, T_obs = 3 s; AI: mean of five seeds) for the detuned passive TLCD, the original RS-TLCD, the constant pre-set RS-TLCD, the AI preset and the Oracle.

**Fig. 5.** (a) Mean reduction over the eleven records for each of the 49 (frequency, angle) cells set from t = 0; the star marks the best constant. (b) Dependence on the angle at the two ends of the tunable range; the dashed line is the Oracle mean.

**Fig. 6.** Difference between the AI setting and the constant pre-set setting for each building frequency and observation time T_obs, mean ± standard error over the seven earthquakes (AI preset and AI online).
